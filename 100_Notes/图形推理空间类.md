## 相邻面
> [!note]
> 三个面挨在一起 -> 优先==公共点==
> 三个面没有挨在一起 -> 优先==公共边==

![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/zxcfdcmv/notes/images/assets/20260916T035115860Z.png)

### 公共边
1. 平面图中==构成直角==的两条边是同一条边
   ![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/zxcfdcmv/notes/images/assets/20260916T033802610Z.png)
2. 一列/行连着==4个面==，两头的两条边是同一条边
   ![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/zxcfdcmv/notes/images/assets/20260916T033925351Z.png)
3. "3+1 构成L形"，同方向的两条边是同一条边
   ![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/zxcfdcmv/notes/images/assets/20260916T034124591Z.png)

### 公共点
> [!tip] 相邻三个面的公共点是唯一的

![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/zxcfdcmv/notes/images/assets/20260916T035220190Z.png)


### 画边法
1. 结合选项，找一个特殊面的==唯一点==或==唯一边==
   ![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/zxcfdcmv/notes/images/assets/20260916T035744605Z.png)
2. 顺/逆时针方向描边标号（描同一个面）
   ![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/zxcfdcmv/notes/images/assets/20260916T035931092Z.png)
   ![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/zxcfdcmv/notes/images/assets/20260916T040105755Z.png)
3. 题干与选项对应面不一致 -> ==排除==
